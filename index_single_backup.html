About Me

Turning search intent into measurable, organic growth.

I'm Anil Kumar Yadav, a Digital Marketing Executive with 1.5+ years of hands-on experience helping businesses grow their organic search presence. My work spans SEO strategy, Technical SEO audits, content optimization and search performance tracking — always tied back to real ranking and traffic outcomes.

I have a genuine passion for how search engines work — from crawl budgets and Core Web Vitals to how AI-powered search is reshaping discovery. That curiosity keeps me constantly testing, measuring and refining, backed by a strong analytical mindset and a habit of learning something new every week.

Alongside my professional work, I'm currently pursuing my Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Information Technology (4th year), which gives me a technical edge when auditing site architecture, debugging indexing issues, or speaking the same language as development teams.
